# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Young people who attend the events and want to discover the music, visual work,
and relevant event information.

## Product Purpose

Benni presents an art and picture portfolio alongside electronic techno and
German rap event information. It should help visitors understand the creative
world, explore the work, find important dates, and get in contact.

## Positioning

An abstract, cool creative world influenced by rap, hip hop, trap, and Chief
Keef, connecting visual portfolio work with the event experience.

## Operating Context

Visitors use the site to explore artwork and images, check event data and
important dates, and find a contact route before or after attending an event.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is prerendered to static HTML and has no runtime backend.
- It needs to support German and English content.
- It should present artwork, images, event data, important dates, and contact
  information.
- The current repository contains a home page and an Impressum route; existing
  copy and contact details are placeholders.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the initial site shell, favicon, home page, Impressum,
and placeholder metadata. No confirmed project portfolio, event schedule,
social links, or final contact details are present yet. Future work must not
invent these materials.

## Product Principles

- Make the creative work and event experience easy to discover.
- Keep event information and important dates reliable and prominent.
- Connect visual art, music, and audience context rather than presenting them
  as unrelated sections.
- Keep contact information easy to find.
- Preserve static, fast, low-maintenance delivery.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is Benni.
- The creative direction is abstract, cool, and influenced by rap, hip hop,
  trap, and Chief Keef.
- Content must support German and English.
